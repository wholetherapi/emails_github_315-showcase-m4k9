BERT-Sentiment fine-tunes a pre-trained BERT encoder for aspect-based sentiment classification. The work describes the auxiliary sentence-pair construction, the aspect-aware pooling layer and the calibration procedure used to obtain competitive results on the SemEval benchmarks.
